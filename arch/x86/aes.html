AES - Advanced Encryption Standard

Instruction

  • AESENC

  • AESENCLAST

  • AESDEC

  • AESDECLAST

  • AESKEYGENASSIST

  • AESIMC

Headers

#include <vaesintrin.h>
#include <wmmintrin.h>

关闭虚拟机`sse`特性

<domain type='kvm' xmlns:qemu='http://libvirt.org/schemas/domain/qemu/1.0'>
  ...
  <qemu:commandline>
    <qemu:arg value='-cpu'/>
    <qemu:arg value='host,-aes'/>
  </qemu:commandline>
</domain>

或者

<domain type='kvm' xmlns:qemu='http://libvirt.org/schemas/domain/qemu/1.0'>
  ...
  <cpu mode='host-model' check='none'>
    <feature policy='disable' name='vmx'/>
  </cpu>
  ...
  <qemu:commandline>
    <qemu:arg value='-cpu'/>
    <qemu:arg value='host,-aes'/>
  </qemu:commandline>
</domain>
