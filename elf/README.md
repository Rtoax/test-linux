ELF (Executable and Linkable Format)
======================================


- `.rel.plt` are `R_386_JUMP_SLOT`
- `.rela.plt` are `R_X86_64_JUMP_SLOT`
- `.rel.dyn` are `R_386_GLOB_DAT`
- `.rela.dyn` are `R_X86_64_GLOB_DAT`
