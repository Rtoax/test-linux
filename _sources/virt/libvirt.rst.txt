Libvirt
=======

File Location
-------------

- `log`: `/var/log/libvirt/qemu/VM_NAME.log`
- `cpu_map`: `/usr/share/libvirt/cpu_map/`
- xml: `/var/lib/libvirt/qemu/` or `/etc/libvirt/qemu/`
